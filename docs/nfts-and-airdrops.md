# NFT collections and creator airdrops

Give your project's identity more ways to reach people.

## NFT collections

[SPAWN NFTs](https://spawnmeme.com/nfts) supports Metaplex Core collections and public mints, with artwork and metadata stored on Arweave.

Develop the artwork and collection concept first. Review the name, description, traits, supply, mint settings and costs in the site before approving the creation flow. Use the [NFT guide](https://spawnmeme.com/nft-guide) for current steps.

A one-of-one artwork should be described consistently with its actual mint configuration. An image marked “1/1” does not by itself enforce an on-chain supply of one.

Describe what a collectible represents. Do not promise ownership rights, benefits or utility that have not been granted or implemented.

## Creator airdrops

The [Airdrop page](https://spawnmeme.com/airdrop) lets wallets join the creator-distribution whitelist. The [creator sending tool](https://spawnmeme.com/airdrop/send) supports distributing creator-supplied tokens to eligible recipients under the current selection rules.

Creators review the token, recipients, amounts and transaction costs before approval. Joining a whitelist does not guarantee selection or an allocation, and distributed tokens may have no value.

## Keep links clear

Share the exact collection or project page. Publish the full mint or collection address when appropriate. Do not ask community members to send seed phrases or private keys.

## Airdrop fees and collection sharing

Creator airdrops carry a **0.001 SOL per recipient** SPAWN fee, plus distributed tokens, network fees and applicable recipient account creation. See [the airdrop guide](feature-guides/community-and-rewards/23-send-airdrops.md).

NFT collection pages support [Share links](feature-guides/platform-information/45-share-links.md) with collection-specific previews and [Report](feature-guides/reference-and-support/46-report.md) for moderation concerns.
