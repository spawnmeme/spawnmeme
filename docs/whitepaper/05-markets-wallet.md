# 5. Markets, trading and wallet tools

## Discovery inside and beyond SPAWN

Spawn Feed focuses on projects launched through the platform. Markets expands discovery to wider Solana activity, including trending tokens, gainers, losers, volume leaders and new pools. These views help users find assets, but their scope and data sources differ.

A market rank is a data signal. A listing or verification label does not establish investment quality, suitability or the credibility of every claim made by an issuer. Data can lag, and new or thinly traded assets may have incomplete information.

## Inspecting an asset

A token page connects available market information with the token's full mint and relevant actions. Users should compare the full mint against an official source and use explorer links for on-chain inspection.

Charts depend on coverage and supported pools. A missing chart is not proof that an asset is safe or unsafe; it indicates that the interface does not currently provide that view. A visible chart also does not guarantee an executable swap.

Where tokenized assets appear, their issuer terms, access conditions and underlying rights require separate review. Displaying a tokenized stock instrument does not establish ordinary shareholder rights for its holder.

## Spawn Swap

Spawn Swap uses Jupiter routing across available supported liquidity, including Meteora and Raydium routes where available. Quotes depend on the asset, amount, liquidity and current conditions.

Before approval, the user should review the input and output assets, expected output, slippage, minimum received and fees. Routes can change or become unavailable. Comparing available routes does not guarantee the best price across every venue at every moment.

SPAWN token-page bonding-curve trades and wider-market swaps are separate flows. Their fees, referral treatment and current support should not be conflated.

## My Wallet, Explorer and Incinerator

My Wallet brings balances, searchable holdings, recent activity and receive or trading shortcuts into the same interface. It is a view of the connected wallet and supported actions, not a transfer of control over that wallet.

Explorer supports token, wallet and transaction lookup. Incinerator supports burning unwanted tokens and closing eligible token accounts to reclaim rent. Burning destroys the selected tokens; recovering account rent does not recover their purchase price.

These tools reduce navigation between services while leaving transaction review with the wallet owner.

## Limit orders and holder inspection

A swap uses a current quote, with funds in the wallet until the transaction executes. A limit order waits for the selected dollar price with funds deposited in your own Jupiter vault. SPAWN does not hold the deposit. Expired orders require withdrawal; cancellation also requires completing the withdrawal. Only the connected owner wallet can deposit or withdraw.

[Limit orders](../feature-guides/discover-and-trade/42-limit-orders.md) have a $10 minimum, a maximum 30-day expiry and no SPAWN fee; network and Jupiter fees may apply. [Top holders](../feature-guides/discover-and-trade/44-top-holders.md) adds creator holdings and top-ten concentration to token-page inspection. Wallet count is not a count of independent people.
