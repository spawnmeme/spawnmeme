# 44. Top holders: check a token before you buy

See who holds a token, how much the creator holds, and how concentrated the supply is.

**Open:** any token page on [the Spawn Feed](https://spawnmeme.com/launchpad) and scroll to **Top holders**.

## What the card shows

* **Creator holds:** the creator wallet's current share of supply, and how much it bought at launch. "Not in top 20" means the creator's wallet is not among the 20 largest holders; it does not mean their balance is zero.
* **Top 10 wallets:** how much the ten largest wallets hold together. The bonding-curve pool is not counted.
* **The list:** the 20 largest holders, read from Solana, with the **Bonding curve** pool and the **Creator** labelled. Tap an address to open it on Solscan. Press **Show all** to see past the first ten.

Shares are of total supply. The card refreshes after you trade on the page.

## How to read it

1. Early in a token's life the bonding curve holds most of the supply. That is normal.
2. Look at the creator's share and compare it with what they bought at launch. A lower balance can reflect sales or transfers; the card alone does not establish the creator's intent.
3. Look at the top 10 share. A high figure means a few wallets could move the price.
4. Check the mint address, the curve progress and recent trades as well.

One person can control several wallets, so treat the card as a guide and not a guarantee.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "Holder data is unavailable right now" | Reload the page in a moment. |
| "Could not refresh just now; showing the last result" | The last successful reading is shown. Reload to try again. |
| The creator is not labelled | The creator wallet is not among the 20 largest holders. |

**Success:** you can see the creator's share and the top 10 share before you decide.
