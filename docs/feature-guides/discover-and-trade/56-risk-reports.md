# 56. Read RugCheck reports and holder maps

**Open:** a launched token page from [Spawn Feed](https://spawnmeme.com/launchpad).

Use the full mint address, **Top holders**, **RugCheck risk report**, lock information and independent records together when inspecting a token.

## Read the report

1. Find **RugCheck risk report**, when a report is available for that mint.
2. Read the displayed score and each **Danger**, **Warning** or **Note**, including its description.
3. If the token remains on its bonding curve, read the liquidity explanation. The interface notes that conventional pool-liquidity checks may not count the bonding curve; this context does not erase other warnings.
4. Choose **Open the full report on RugCheck** to review the source report.
5. Compare its findings with Top holders, creator activity, authorities and the current token/pool records.

SPAWN displays RugCheck's independent findings rather than providing its own audit. A low score or no flags is not a safety guarantee. A missing report means no report is displayed; it does not mean the token passed.

## Holder maps are conditional

A **Holder map** powered by Bubblemaps is shown only when the required partner configuration and provider coverage are available. If the card is present, choose **Show the holder map** to load it. Wallet-transfer links can reveal relationships, but they do not prove the identities or common ownership of those wallets.

Do not expect an embedded map on every token. New assets can lack provider coverage. Top holders and external inspection links remain useful without the map.

## Troubleshooting

If the card or map is unavailable, check the full mint on the external provider and try later. Do not treat unavailable data as a zero-risk result. **Success:** you can explain the displayed findings and their limits before deciding whether to trade.
