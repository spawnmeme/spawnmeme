# 30. Organic Trending and paid promotion

**Open:** [SPAWN Trending](https://spawnmeme.com/trending) and the [central Telegram channel](https://t.me/spawntrending). The channel carries the pinned, updating board; project groups receive their own connected Buy Bot alerts.

## Read organic ranks

The **Top activity** board uses a rolling 60-minute window for supported connected bonding-curve tokens. Qualification requires at least two buyers. Ranking considers unique buyers, buy volume and buy count; creator buys are excluded and repeat-wallet influence is capped.

This covers the monitored set, not every Solana token. It cannot identify every related wallet. Use the board's update time and refresh control. When monitoring is stale, ranks are hidden rather than presented as current.

When a connected token qualifies, its Buy Bot alerts can show a current rank badge. Buying a sponsored slot does not purchase an organic rank.

## Buy sponsored visibility

1. Select a supported token or paste its mint on the Trending page.
2. In **Promote your token**, review the available durations and SOL prices.
3. Connect the paying wallet and sign ownership verification when requested.
4. Choose a package and **Pay with SOL**.
5. Review the generated transaction. It pays SPAWN's designated Solana wallet with an order-specific memo/reference.
6. Approve once and keep the page open while the receipt is checked.
7. Confirm **Your promotion is active**, or the message that payment is verified and queued for publication.

Paid time starts when the campaign is published. Promoted entries rotate and appear separately from activity ranks. A connected alert may show a **Promoted** badge. The purchase guarantees no number of views, buyers, trades or returns.

Use the site's order flow. A Telegram channel URL is not a Solana receiving address, and an unsolicited manual transfer cannot be assumed to activate a campaign.

## Recover payment

| State                                      | Correct action                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Pending payment receipt** with signature | Use **Check payment**. Do not send SOL again.                                                           |
| Paid but signature not filled              | Paste the full signature from your wallet into that order and check it.                                 |
| Browser lost the local order               | Connect the same payer and use **Recover my orders**. Open **Check this order's payment** when offered. |
| Unpaid quote and wallet sent nothing       | **Discard unpaid quote** is appropriate only after confirming no payment was sent.                      |
| Payment verified, queued                   | Wait for board publication; paid time has not started merely because the payment was accepted.          |
| **Paid placements are not open yet**       | Payments are disabled; do not send funds directly.                                                      |

## Other troubleshooting

No organic rank can mean insufficient recent qualifying buyers, an unsupported state, no connected monitoring or stale data. A rank can change as old activity leaves the 60-minute window. An expired sponsored order is not a payment failure. For unresolved paid orders, provide order ID, mint, payer and signature to [support](../reference-and-support/39-support-and-safety.md).

## Token refresh and private alerts

The shared five-minute refresh records supported tokens’ SOL raised and market cap, marks graduation when the curve completes, and sends [private token alerts](43-token-alerts.md). Price alerts stop at graduation; Curve milestones can notify the graduation event. This does not extend Buy Bot trade monitoring to graduated pools. Refresh delays can delay notifications.
