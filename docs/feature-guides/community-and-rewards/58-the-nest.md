# 58. Join The Nest with a wallet

**Open:** a published project page or a linked token page and find **The Nest**. On a token page it sits below the chart and recent trades when a project is linked.

Everyone can read and share. You can participate using a verified wallet or an email account.

## React and post

1. Choose **Connect wallet**, or **Verify** if connected already. Sign the free ownership message. This sends no transaction and does not spend SOL.
2. Alternatively choose **or sign in with email**.
3. Select **Hype**, **Love** or **Watching**. Choose another to switch, or tap the selected reaction to remove it.
4. Optionally enter a nickname, up to 24 characters.
5. Write a message of 2–280 characters and choose **Post**. Keep it friendly and omit links, addresses and personal details.
6. Use **Show all messages** to expand an embedded conversation, or **Share project** to share its page.

For a launched token, wallet-based reactions require holding that token; the interface also permits signed-in accounts. A verified participant can post a message without holding it. A connected wallet alone is insufficient until ownership verification succeeds.

## Labels and moderation

Wallet posts show a shortened wallet address. A **Holder** label reflects holdings at posting time, not a permanent balance guarantee. **Project creator** identifies the creator in the conversation.

Use **Remove** on your own message. Authorized project creators and SPAWN administrators can also hide messages. A nickname is not proof of a person's identity.

## Recovery

If your verification expires or you change wallets, verify the intended wallet again. If The Nest is unavailable, sharing can still work; wait before resubmitting an uncertain post. Community reactions and messages express members' opinions and are not trading signals.

**Success:** your reaction updates or your message appears under the intended identity.
