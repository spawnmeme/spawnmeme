# 53. Leaderboards and activity badges

**Open:** [SPAWN Leaderboard](https://spawnmeme.com/leaderboard).

The public activity board has four tabs. It is separate from Spawn Points and the Quests page's participation ranking.

| Tab | Ranking shown | Useful controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Traders | Recorded SOL volume on SPAWN bonding curves, buys and sells together | Last 7 days or All time |
| Creators | Trading volume their launches attract from other wallets; the creator wallet's own trades on its own token are excluded | Creator page and token links |
| Referrers | New traders brought in through a referral link | Referred-trader count and their trading volume |
| Tokens | Market cap among the listed results | Open the token page |

## Use the board

1. Select the relevant tab. On **Traders**, select the time period.
2. Compare the ranking metric with the accompanying trade, token or launch counts.
3. Open the wallet on Solscan or the creator/token page for context.
4. Optionally connect your wallet to highlight your row with **You**.

## Badge meanings

* **Creator of the week:** most trading from other wallets on their launches during the last seven days.
* **Top trader of the week:** most SOL traded during the last seven days.
* **Top referrer:** most new traders brought to SPAWN.
* **Graduated a token:** launched a token that completed its curve.
* **Locks supply:** tokens remain locked on at least one launch.

Weekly badges change with the rolling period. Rankings refresh every few minutes and carry no reward. They do not measure profitability. Volume can be inflated, and excluding a creator's own address does not identify every address that person controls.

If the board cannot load, refresh later. An empty tab means there are no results available for that view, not that every wallet has no activity. **Success:** you understand the selected metric and can inspect the underlying wallet or launch.
