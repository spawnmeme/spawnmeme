# 25. Refer & Earn

**Open:** [Refer and earn](https://spawnmeme.com/refer). The referral link and payouts are tied to a wallet; no email signup is required.

## Get and share your link

1. Choose **Connect wallet to get my link**.
2. Confirm the **Paid to** address.
3. Use **Copy** to copy the generated link exactly, preserving its referral information.
4. Share it with people who may want to use SPAWN, while clearly describing that you may earn from qualifying activity.
5. Return to review **Referred wallets**, **Their volume**, **Earned** and **Paid so far**.

Records are derived from qualifying trades read from Solana and can lag by a few minutes.

## What qualifies

The current published terms give the referrer 25% of the fee SPAWN itself receives on eligible referred bonding-curve trades for 90 days from that wallet's first qualifying trade. Check the live page for current terms.

The referred wallet must be new to SPAWN, and its first eligible referral attribution counts. Trades on SPAWN token pages qualify under the program rules. Spawn Swap, other apps and trades after graduation do not qualify for this platform referral payout. Self-referrals and abusive activity do not earn rewards.

The share is a percentage of SPAWN's received fee, **not 25% of trade value**. Payouts are sent periodically in SOL after the owed amount reaches the current minimum, published as 0.01 SOL at this review. There is no fixed instant payout schedule for this balance.

## Optional instant bonus

The page also exposes a separate Meteora on-chain referral bonus. When **Instant bonus: off** appears, **Switch on the instant bonus** can create the required wrapped-SOL account with a wallet transaction and account rent.

This bonus follows the browser's remembered referral link and requires the receiving wrapped-SOL account. It is separate from SPAWN's Earned/Paid figures. Unwrapping or closing that account can switch the bonus off; recheck the page afterward. Review the current bonus details and costs before approving setup.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem                        | Resolution                                                                               |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Wrong payout address           | Connect the intended wallet before copying and distributing a link.                      |
| Clicks but no referred wallets | A click alone is not a qualifying trade and wallet attribution.                          |
| Trade absent                   | Check program coverage, first-referral rules, wallet eligibility and indexing delay.     |
| Earned but not paid            | Check the minimum and periodic payout rules; keep receipts and ask support if necessary. |
| Instant bonus disappeared      | Check whether the wrapped-SOL account was closed or unwrapped.                           |
| Friend used another browser    | Browser referral information may not follow them automatically.                          |

Copying a link is free. Optional bonus account setup and referred trades have their normal costs. Rewards depend on eligible real activity, not a guaranteed income stream.

## Share a token with your referral code

On a token page, connect your referral wallet and use **Copy my referral link**. The copied link includes your code and previews the token’s own artwork and details. Eligible attribution still follows the referral rules. See [45. Share links that preview the page](../platform-information/45-share-links.md).


## Creator fees and referral calculations

Creator earnings come from the existing curve-fee split. Referral rewards continue to use the fee SPAWN itself receives after applicable deductions, rather than 25% of the entire 1.2% trading fee. A pool’s creator setting affects SPAWN’s remaining share. See [50. Claim your creator fees](../launch-and-manage-tokens/50-claim-creator-fees.md).
