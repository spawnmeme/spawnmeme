# 22. Join the airdrop whitelist

**Open:** [Airdrop whitelist](https://spawnmeme.com/airdrop). Joining gives your wallet a chance to receive tokens from creators who choose to distribute them.

## Eligibility

At joining time, the wallet must hold some official $SPAWN or have a qualifying prior trade on SPAWN. The page's **Check again** action checks your current status. A failed balance lookup is not the same as a confirmed ineligible balance.

The interface directs qualifying trading through SPAWN token pages. Do not assume activity on another app or Spawn Swap satisfies the check. There is no need to trade repeatedly to join.

## Join

1. Choose **Connect wallet** and confirm the address where you want tokens delivered.
2. Wait for the eligibility check.
3. If eligible, choose **Join the whitelist**.
4. Sign the free wallet-ownership message when requested.
5. Confirm **is on the whitelist** or **You are on the whitelist**.

If your wallet is drawn for a creator's distribution, the tokens arrive directly. You do not need to connect to a claim site, pay a claim fee or sign again to receive that distribution.

## Leave

Reconnect the same wallet and choose **Take my wallet off the list**. Sign ownership verification if requested. This removes future whitelist participation; it does not undo tokens already received or erase public blockchain records.

## Expectations and costs

Joining is free and uses a message, not a payment transaction. One entry is maintained per wallet. Creators decide whether and how much to send, and recipients are drawn at random. Participation guarantees neither selection nor token value. Eligibility is checked when joining; the page explains that you stay listed afterward unless removed or you leave.

## Troubleshooting

| Message                           | What to do                                                                                                 |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cannot join yet                   | Read which condition is missing. Confirm that the balance/trade belongs to this wallet.                    |
| Balance could not be read         | Wait and choose **Check again**; do not treat it as proof you need to buy.                                 |
| Recent trade not counted          | Allow a few minutes for indexing and recheck.                                                              |
| Removed by SPAWN                  | The page indicates that this wallet cannot rejoin. Ask support about an error if you believe one occurred. |
| Signed but no joined confirmation | Refresh/recheck status with the same wallet before repeating.                                              |
| No airdrops received              | Selection is not guaranteed. There may be no active creator distribution.                                  |

Treat unsolicited “claim your SPAWN airdrop” links as unrelated to this direct-distribution flow. [Creators can send airdrops here](23-send-airdrops.md).
