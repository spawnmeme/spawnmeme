## How to use this manual

Open the linked feature on the official site, check its prerequisites, and follow the numbered steps. Bold text identifies controls seen in the reviewed interface. Replace clearly marked example mints, URLs and amounts with your intended values. Do not paste placeholder addresses into a payment or launch.

Wallet connection, a free message signature and a paid transaction are different steps. If an action produced a transaction signature, inspect it before repeating the action. Pending receipts and upload progress may depend on the original browser's saved state.

## Version and availability

**Edition 1.2 · Updated 10 October 2026.** This edition incorporates the October 9 release package and the October 10 creator-fee guide, with the fee split and claiming flow checked against the current site code. They are documentation, not a claim that every wallet/device combination or paid workflow was independently exercised on mainnet.

Current activation settings, wallet ownership, project access and provider coverage can limit a feature. Live quotes, status messages and [News & Updates](https://spawnmeme.com/updates) take precedence when the product changes. Known restrictions are stated in the relevant guide, including custom-domain availability, analytics access, supported Buy Bot curves and reported SafePal Android issues.

The [administrator publishing guide](platform-administration/41-publish-updates.md) is intended only for authorized platform staff. All other guides are organized around creator, trader and community tasks.
