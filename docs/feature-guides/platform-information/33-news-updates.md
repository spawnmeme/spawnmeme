# 33. Read and share News & Updates

**Open:** [News & Updates](https://spawnmeme.com/updates), or send `/updates` to the Telegram bot.

## Find an announcement

1. Open the news page and review the latest featured update.
2. Filter by **All**, **Platform**, **Wallets**, **Community** or **Promotions**.
3. Choose **Read the update** or the article title.
4. Check its category and publication date, then read the complete article.
5. Use **Older updates** or **Newer updates** where pagination is offered.

## Share it

Open the article and choose **Copy article link**. Share that exact URL so readers reach the full official context. If clipboard permission fails, copy the page address manually.

Use **Follow in Telegram** or **Join SPAWN on Telegram** to open the official community. Site publication and Telegram notification are separate actions: an article may exist without a Telegram notice. Buy Bot token alerts are also separate from platform news.

## How to interpret announcements

Use the date and current feature screen together. A promotion can expire, an old wallet workaround can become outdated, and a planned feature can remain unavailable. A roadmap entry is not the same as a published release announcement. For current payments and activation, rely on the relevant live tool's quote and status.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem                                  | Resolution                                                                                      |
| ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| No updates in a filter                   | Select **All**; that category may have no published articles.                                   |
| Could not load updates                   | Use **Try again** after checking connectivity.                                                  |
| Update not found                         | Check the complete URL. It may be unpublished or have an incorrect slug. Return to All updates. |
| Telegram notice missing                  | Not every publication sends a notice; read the official news page.                              |
| Image or link differs from a copied post | Use the official article as the reference and report a broken destination.                      |

Reading and sharing are free and require no wallet or account. Platform staff can use the separate [publishing guide](../platform-administration/41-publish-updates.md).

## Copy a preview-ready article link

Use **Copy article link** on the article. It copies a share link that previews the article’s own title and cover image. Browser-address links may use the generic SPAWN card, and apps may cache older previews. See [45. Share links that preview the page](45-share-links.md).
