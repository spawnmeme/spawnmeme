**Current reference:** [Platform Fees & Economics](https://spawnmeme.com/fees). Exact live quotes and wallet transaction review take precedence over a static guide.

## Read a transaction before approving

1. Confirm the site, feature and selected wallet.
2. Compare the quoted asset/amount and intended token mint.
3. Check network fees, account creation and optional purchases separately.
4. For a service purchase, let the site build its payment reference. Do not replace it with an unrelated manual transfer.
5. Save the order ID and transaction signature after submission.

A signature means you can inspect the transaction; it does not by itself prove success. A failed submitted Solana transaction may still incur a network fee. A successful service payment can precede the site's confirmation or moderation step.

## Avoid duplicate payments

If SOL left the wallet or a signature exists, use the relevant recovery action: **Retry registration**, **Check payment**, **Check my payment**, **Retry verification**, or the NFT/airdrop continuation control. Check the receipt before starting over. Only discard an unpaid quote when you have verified that the wallet sent nothing.

## Common misunderstandings

“Free mint” can mean zero creator price while platform/network costs remain. “Free deployment” can mean only the platform launch fee is waived. Account rent is separate from trading value, and may be recoverable only for eligible accounts under the relevant rules. A paid promotion is visibility, not a token purchase or return guarantee.

If the wallet amount materially differs from the quote and you cannot explain it, stop before signing and ask [support](39-support-and-safety.md) with the page and quote details.

## Limit-order costs

[Limit orders](../discover-and-trade/42-limit-orders.md) carry **no SPAWN fee**. The minimum order is **$10**. Solana network fees and any Jupiter fees apply. Deposited order funds are trade principal, not a SPAWN service payment.
