# News & Updates

[Fresh from the SPAWN lab](https://spawnmeme.com/updates) brings platform announcements into one place.

## Read and share

- Browse recent articles or filter by Platform, Wallets, Community and Promotions.
- Open an article for the full announcement and its individual link.
- Use **Copy article link** to share a specific update.
- Open the page from the site's Learn menu and footer, or use `/updates` in the SPAWN Telegram bot.

The initial articles cover the upgraded buybot, MetaMask support, wallet-only launches, live feed improvements and Launch Week. A promotion article remains an archive record after its stated offer window ends.

## Publishing for SPAWN administrators

The admin dashboard includes a News & announcements editor. Save a draft, preview the article, then publish it to the site. Drafts are private; published articles are publicly readable. Administrators can edit or unpublish a post.

New posts can optionally notify the official connected SPAWN Telegram group with a title, summary and **Read the update** button. This option is off by default. A published article can also be announced separately from the editor.

Editing an article does not automatically resend its notice. Delivery state prevents duplicate sends; if delivery is uncertain, check the group before taking further action. Historical articles are not automatically sent as a batch.
