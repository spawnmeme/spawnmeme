# Markets, swaps and your wallet

SPAWN connects discovery, inspection and wallet actions in one platform.

## Discover

[Markets](https://spawnmeme.com/markets) covers wider Solana discovery: trending tokens, gainers, losers, volume leaders and new pools. The [Spawn Feed](https://spawnmeme.com/launchpad) focuses on SPAWN launches.

Supported tokenized assets may also appear in Markets. Availability and eligibility vary. Tokenized stock exposure does not itself provide ordinary shareholder rights.

A listing, verification label or market rank is a discovery signal, not a guarantee about a project. Market data can lag.

## A more responsive Spawn Feed

The homepage feed and dedicated Spawn Feed share their launch data. Realtime updates are backed by periodic refreshes, helping newly recorded launches appear across both views.

Cards can briefly highlight when new buy activity is recorded. Duplicate events are filtered. Confirmation and indexing can introduce delays; a visual highlight is not a promise of immediate execution or future performance.

## Inspect

Open the token's page and check its full mint address. Review available market information and follow external on-chain records when you need more detail.

Use [Explorer](https://spawnmeme.com/explorer) to look up a Solana token, wallet or transaction. A familiar logo or ticker is not sufficient verification.

## Review a swap

[Spawn Swap](https://spawnmeme.com/spawn-swap) uses Jupiter routing across available supported liquidity, including Meteora and Raydium routes where available.

Quotes depend on the asset, liquidity and current market conditions. Read the input/output tokens, expected output, slippage and wallet transaction before approval. A displayed quote does not guarantee that a route will remain available.

SPAWN token-page bonding-curve trades and wider-market swaps are different flows; consult the current fee details for the flow you use.

## Choose your Solana wallet

SPAWN offers Phantom, Solflare, Backpack, SafePal and MetaMask in its wallet selector. MetaMask uses its Solana connection flow, including supported injected-wallet and mobile/QR options. Choose **MetaMask** explicitly and approve the connection in the intended wallet.

Wallet options depend on the device, wallet version and browser. SafePal on Android has had loading/freezing reports, so compatibility across all Android wallet browsers should not be assumed. For a problem report, include the page, device, wallet version and browser through [Help](https://spawnmeme.com/help).

Token deployment supports wallet verification without an email signup. Verification messages and transaction approvals serve separate purposes, and both remain in your wallet.

## Follow your wallet

[My Wallet](https://spawnmeme.com/wallet) lets you view balances, search holdings, review recent activity and open receive or trading shortcuts.

The [Incinerator](https://spawnmeme.com/burn) supports burning unwanted tokens and closing eligible token accounts to reclaim rent. Burning is irreversible; inspect the selected assets carefully before approving.

## Limit orders and token inspection

[Limit orders](feature-guides/discover-and-trade/42-limit-orders.md) let you choose a dollar price for a SOL/token order, with a $10 minimum and expiry up to 30 days. Depositing transfers funds from your wallet to your own Jupiter vault; SPAWN does not hold them. Expired or cancelled orders require completing withdrawal. SPAWN charges no limit-order fee; network and Jupiter fees can apply, and a fill is not guaranteed.

Token pages now include [Top holders](feature-guides/discover-and-trade/44-top-holders.md), [private Alerts](feature-guides/telegram-and-promotion/43-token-alerts.md), [Share](feature-guides/platform-information/45-share-links.md) and [Report](feature-guides/reference-and-support/46-report.md).

If MetaMask reports that an account or method is not authorized, SPAWN asks it to reconnect and retries. If needed, disconnect, select MetaMask again, approve the Solana account and retry. This authorization failure sends no transaction and charges nothing. Start other service troubleshooting with [Status](feature-guides/reference-and-support/48-status.md).
