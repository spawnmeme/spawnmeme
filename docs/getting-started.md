# Getting started with SPAWN

Start at [spawnmeme.com/start](https://spawnmeme.com/start) and choose the path that fits your project.

## Build from an idea

1. Describe the character, theme or community you want to create.
2. Use Spawn AI to explore a name, story and artwork.
3. Review and edit the output so it represents your project accurately.
4. Build a public project page and explore the optional website and social tools.
5. When ready, review the token launch details, costs and wallet transaction.
6. Share the confirmed project and full mint address with your community.

A website is optional. Creators with an existing identity can go directly to [Deploy a Token](https://spawnmeme.com/create-token).

## Explore and trade

Use [Markets](https://spawnmeme.com/markets) for wider Solana discovery or the [Spawn Feed](https://spawnmeme.com/launchpad) for launched projects. Compare the full mint address before reviewing a quote in [Spawn Swap](https://spawnmeme.com/spawn-swap).

[My Wallet](https://spawnmeme.com/wallet) brings balances, holdings and recent activity into the platform. You approve supported on-chain actions in your wallet.

On a phone, use your wallet's browser when its connection flow requires it. Account requirements vary by feature; wallet-based tools do not all require an email account.

## Build beyond launch

- Open the [creator dashboard](https://spawnmeme.com/creator-dashboard) with the wallet you launched from.
- Connect your Telegram using the [Buy Bot guide](buy-bot-and-trending.md).
- Explore [NFT collections and airdrops](nfts-and-airdrops.md).
- Join [The Nest](https://t.me/spawnmemetg) and follow [SPAWN on Medium](https://medium.com/@spawnmeme).
- Explore [The Hatching Plan](https://spawnmeme.com/roadmap) for the platform's direction.

## Before approving a transaction

Review the network, asset, full mint address, amount and fees. Keep enough SOL for network costs. Never share a seed phrase or private key with the bot, a support account or a GitHub issue.

Use [Help](https://spawnmeme.com/help), [Fees](https://spawnmeme.com/fees) and [How launches work](https://spawnmeme.com/how-it-works) for current details.
