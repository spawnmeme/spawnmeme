## Community participation

[Quests](https://spawnmeme.com/quests) includes daily check-ins, streaks and creation, wallet and community tasks. Spawn Points are off-chain, non-transferable, and have no cash or token value. No future airdrop is guaranteed.

[Refer & earn](https://spawnmeme.com/refer) provides referral links for the program's eligible trades. Check the page for supported flows, attribution, exclusions and current reward terms. Quest invitations and trading referrals are separate programs.

## Keep the community useful

Share your work, ask specific questions and provide constructive feedback. Avoid spam, impersonation, price promises and unsolicited token promotion.

Check the current official token mint on the website and with `/ca` in the main SPAWN group. Admins and the bot never need your seed phrase or private key.
