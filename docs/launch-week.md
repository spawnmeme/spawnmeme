# Launch Week — October 2026

**Offer window:** October 8, 2026 at 11:35 a.m. EDT to October 15, 2026 at 11:35 a.m. EDT (15:35 UTC on both dates).

This is documentation of a dated promotion, not a permanent free-deployment policy. Check the [Launch Week page](https://spawnmeme.com/launch-week) for the current status and countdown.

## What is waived

During the stated offer window, SPAWN waives its **0.025 SOL platform deployment fee** for eligible deployments completed within the promotion terms.

## What still costs SOL

- Solana network fees.
- Account creation costs.
- Any optional first buy.

Trading fees are separate from the deployment offer. The waiver does not make all on-chain activity free.

## How to use it

1. Review the current offer status on the dedicated page.
2. Open [Deploy a Token](https://spawnmeme.com/create-token).
3. Connect and verify the intended wallet; email signup is not required for the wallet-only flow.
4. Review the actual fee quote and transaction before approval.
5. Follow the quote expiry and confirmation instructions shown by the app.

Use `/promo` in the SPAWN bot to view the offer status and remaining time. After the offer ends, consult the current launch quote and [Fees page](https://spawnmeme.com/fees).
